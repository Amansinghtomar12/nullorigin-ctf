A signal is coming in from beyond Earth.

It is encrypted, it is getting stronger, and it answers to whoever decodes it first.

Space CTF is a 48-hour Jeopardy-style capture-the-flag built around the systems that keep satellites running: mission control software, radio links, flight computers and the orbital mechanics that tie them together. Start at the ground station, follow the signal up, and capture every flag between here and the Moon.

ALT 408 KMLow Earth orbit

Seven orbits, from the ground station to the Moon.

Challenges are grouped by where they sit in a real space mission. Start on the ground and work your way out.

  1. 0 km · Ground stationWeb

    Ground Segment

    Mission-control dashboards, operator logins and telemetry APIs. Find the bug that hands you the command console.

  2. 8.4 GHz · X-bandCrypto

    Comms Link

    Telecommand ciphers, CCSDS frames and key exchanges between ground and orbit. Break the crypto and forge a command the satellite will trust.

  3. 1200 baud · DownlinkForensics

    Signals

    Packet captures, audio from a satellite overpass and corrupted downlink files. Pull the flag out of the noise.

  4. 408 km · LEOReverse

    Flight Software

    Onboard computer images, a bootloader that hides more than it should, and firmware that talks over I²C. Take it apart.

  5. 550 km · LEOPwn

    Payload

    Camera and payload controllers with memory to corrupt and control flow to take. Get a shell on something moving at 7.6 km/s.

  6. 20,200 km · MEOMisc

    Astrodynamics

    Two-line element sets, visibility windows and az-el tracking. Work out where the satellite is before it drops below the horizon.

  7. 384,400 km · LunarOSINT

    Deep Space

    Launch records, ground-station locations and orbital catalogues. Track down what was meant to stay out of sight.
